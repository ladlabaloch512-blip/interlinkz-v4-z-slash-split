A single page demo built to turn visitors into enquiries for one offer.

This demo shows a landing page: one page built for one offer, such as a course launch, a new service or a seasonal deal. Everything on the page leads to one action, the enquiry form.

A real landing page of this type would carry the business's own offer and would often be linked from ads or social posts. Everything on this page is demo content that shows the layout and the flow.
