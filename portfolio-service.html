Demo Service Website

A website demo for a local service business that wins enquiries.

This demo shows a website for a local service business, such as a repair, cleaning or installation company. The pages explain the services, show past work in a gallery and make it very easy to request a quote.

A real service site of this type would carry the business's own services, work photos and areas covered. Everything on this page is demo content that shows the layout and the flow.

WHAT THIS DEMO INCLUDES

  • Service pages with clear detail on what is included
  • Gallery area for photos of past work
  • Areas covered list so local customers find themselves
  • Quote request form with service and date fields
  • Strong contact buttons on every page
  • Simple structure that loads fast on phones
