OUR PROCESS

How we work, step by step.

Every project follows the same clear path: Plan, Design, Build, Grow. Here is what happens at each step, what you receive and what we need from you.

Plan Design Build Grow

STEP 1 / PLAN

Plan: We start by understanding your business.

We talk about your business, your customers and what your website must do for you. Together we agree the pages, the features and the content the site will carry, so there are no surprises later.

WHAT YOU RECEIVE

A clear written plan: the page list, what each page will carry, the time the work will take and your quotation.

WHAT WE NEED FROM YOU

Your business details, your logo and photos if you have them, and answers to a few short questions.

STEP 2 / DESIGN

Design: You see the design before anything is built.

We design the look of your website first, in your brand colours and style. You review the design screens and tell us what to adjust. Changes are easy at this stage, and the build only starts when you approve the design.

WHAT YOU RECEIVE

Design screens of your main pages, ready for your review and approval.

WHAT WE NEED FROM YOU

Your feedback on the design, and your approval to start the build.

STEP 3 / BUILD

Build: We turn the approved design into a working website.

We build the website page by page and test it on phones and computers. Forms, buttons and links are all checked, and the content is placed exactly as planned.

WHAT YOU RECEIVE

A test link where you can click through the real website before it goes live.

WHAT WE NEED FROM YOU

Your final text and photos, and your approval of the test site.

STEP 4 / GROW

Grow: Your website goes live and keeps improving.

We launch the website on your domain and make sure everything works in the real world. From there we can help with updates, marketing and new pages as your business grows.

WHAT YOU RECEIVE

The live website, your access details where they apply, and a clear support route for changes.

WHAT WE NEED FROM YOU

Your go ahead to launch.
