Demo E-Commerce Store

A compact shop demo for a small boutique that takes orders by message.

This demo shows a smaller, simpler shop website for a boutique or a home based seller. Instead of a full checkout, visitors browse a neat catalogue and send an order or a question by form, which suits shops that confirm orders personally.

A real shop site of this type would carry the seller's own products and photos. Everything on this page is demo content that shows the layout and the flow.

WHAT THIS DEMO INCLUDES

  • Curated catalogue with a clean product grid
  • Product quick look with photo, price and details
  • Simple order form instead of a full checkout
  • Gallery strip in a lookbook style
  • About the maker or the shop section
  • Easy updates when new stock arrives
