EDUCATION

Demo Education Website

An education website demo with courses, course pages and an enrol form.

This demo shows a website for a course provider, academy or training centre. Students can browse courses, open a course page to see what it covers and send an enrol form to join.

A real education site of this type would carry the provider's own courses, teachers and schedule. Everything on this page is demo content that shows the layout and the flow.

WHAT THIS DEMO INCLUDES

  • Course cards with level, format and a short summary
  • Course page with a lesson by lesson list
  • Enrol form with course and contact fields
  • Sample lesson or trial class block
  • About page for the teachers and the method
  • Clear pages for fees and schedules when the provider is ready
